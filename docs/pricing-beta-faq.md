**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Beta Access Program**

**What is the Beta Access Program?**

The Beta Access Program gives early businesses access to the platform
before public launch. Beta members help shape the product and lock in
preferred pricing while we finish building and refining the system.

**Who is this platform designed for?**

The platform is designed primarily for **home services and commercial
service providers**, such as contractors, cleaners, landscapers, and
facility services.

That said, **any professional who accepts client requests** is welcome
to join the beta.

**How much does the beta cost?**

**Beta (Founding Member) Pricing:**

- \$0 setup fee

- \$39 per month

- Includes up to 15 service requests per month

- Additional requests are \$8 each

There are **no surprise charges, auctions, or bidding**.

**How is this different from Angi, HomeAdvisor, or Thumbtack?**

Key differences:

- No shared leads

- No auctions or bidding wars

- Flat monthly pricing

- You control how many requests you receive

- You can pause requests at any time

- No surprise fees

Requests come either directly from your custom link or from customers
browsing the network.

**What counts as a “service request”?**

A service request is a verified inquiry from a prospective client who
has provided:

- Name

- Email

- Phone number

- Service details

No anonymous or incomplete requests are delivered.

**What happens if I reach my monthly request limit?**

Once you reach 15 requests, you choose what happens next:

- Pause requests for the rest of the month, **or**

- Continue receiving requests at \$8 per additional request

You are always in control.

**Can I turn requests off temporarily?**

Yes. You can pause or resume requests at any time with one click. There
are no penalties for pausing.

**Will my price increase after beta ends?**

Founding members keep beta pricing for **at least 12 months** from their
sign‑up date.

After that, standard pricing applies, with notice given well in advance.
There are no surprise increases.

**Is there a long‑term contract?**

No. The beta is month‑to‑month. You can cancel at any time.

**Why do you require my business and contact information?**

We require real business information to:

- Ensure lead quality

- Prevent spam

- Maintain trust across the network

This protects both service providers and clients.

**What do you expect from beta members?**

Beta members are encouraged (but not burdened) to:

- Use the platform actively

- Share feedback

- Help us identify bugs or missing features

Your real‑world input helps shape the product.

**How many beta spots are available?**

Beta access is limited to a fixed number of businesses and will close
once capacity is reached.

**How do I join the beta?**

Complete the beta sign‑up form, enter your payment details, and you’ll
get immediate access to the platform.
